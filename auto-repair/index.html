FAQ

Questions we hear every day

Do I need an appointment?+

For oil changes and inspections, walk-ins are welcome. For repairs, booking guarantees a technician is ready for your car.

Will servicing here void my factory warranty?+

No. Federal law lets you service your car anywhere. We use quality parts and keep records for your warranty.

What parts do you use?+

Original equipment or top-quality aftermarket parts, your choice. Both are covered by our 24/24 warranty.

Do you offer loaner cars?+

Yes, free for repairs that take more than a day, subject to availability. Book ahead to reserve one.

Can I pay over time?+

Yes. Repairs over $500 can be financed for 6 to 12 months, subject to approval.
