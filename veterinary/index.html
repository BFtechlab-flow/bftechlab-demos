FAQ

Questions we hear every day

Do you see rabbits and other small pets?+

Yes. We see dogs, cats, rabbits, guinea pigs and ferrets. For birds and reptiles we'll refer you to a specialist.

Do you offer payment plans?+

Yes, for bills over $300, and we accept most pet insurance plans. Ask us to submit the claim for you.

Can I stay with my pet during the exam?+

Of course, for exams and vaccines. For surgery and dental procedures we'll update you by text.

How do I get my pet's records?+

Ask at the front desk or reply to any reminder text and we'll email them the same day.

What should I bring to the first visit?+

Previous records if you have them, a list of food and medications, and your pet on a leash or in a carrier.
