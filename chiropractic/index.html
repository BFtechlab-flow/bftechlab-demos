FAQ

Questions we hear every day

Does an adjustment hurt?+

Most people feel relief, not pain. If you prefer, we use gentle techniques with no twisting or cracking.

Do I need a referral from my doctor?+

No. You can book directly. We're happy to coordinate with your doctor when it helps your care.

How many visits will I need?+

It depends on your condition. After the first exam you'll get a written plan, and many patients improve in 4 to 6 visits.

Is chiropractic safe?+

Chiropractic care is very safe when performed by a licensed chiropractor after a proper exam, which is how every visit starts here.

What should I wear?+

Comfortable clothes you can move in, like workout clothes. We have gowns if needed.
