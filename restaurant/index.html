Our story

Chef Marco and a family recipe book

Marco Rinaldi learned to roll pasta next to his grandmother in Bologna. In 2009 he opened BF Trattoria with twelve tables and the same recipes.

Today the room is bigger, but the rules are the same: flour from a family mill, tomatoes from a farm ten miles away, and nothing on the menu we wouldn't serve at home.

  • Pasta and bread made fresh every morning
  • Local produce from May to October
  • Wine list built for every budget

FAQ

Questions we hear every day

Do you take walk-ins?+

Yes. The bar and four high-tops are kept for walk-ins every night, first come, first served.

Do you have gluten-free options?+

Gluten-free pasta and pizza crust are available, cooked separately. Tell us about any allergy when you book.

Is there a kids' menu?+

Yes: small pasta, mini pizzas and gelato. High chairs and booster seats are available.

Can I order takeout?+

Order by phone or online for pickup, or through the usual delivery apps within 5 miles.

Do you sell gift cards?+

Yes, at the host stand and online, in any amount.
