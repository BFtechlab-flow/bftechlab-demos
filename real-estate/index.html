FAQ

Questions we hear every day

Do I need to be pre-approved before touring homes?+

It's not required, but it helps you move fast when the right home appears. We can connect you with local lenders.

How do you decide the listing price?+

We study recent sales, active competition and your home's condition, then walk you through the numbers before you decide.

Can you help me buy and sell at the same time?+

Yes. We plan the timing so you're not stuck with two mortgages or nowhere to live.

How long does it take to sell?+

Well-priced homes in our area sell in about three weeks on average, depending on the season and price range.

What does it cost to work with you as a buyer?+

We explain all fees upfront in a written buyer agreement before you tour, so there are no surprises.
