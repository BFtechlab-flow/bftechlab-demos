Questions we get every day

Can I hold a car with a deposit?+

Yes. A refundable deposit holds any vehicle for 48 hours while you arrange financing or a test drive.

Do you deliver?+

Within 60 miles, delivery is free on vehicles over $15,000. Further out, ask us for a quote.

Can my mechanic look at the car first?+

Of course. Take it to your own mechanic for a pre-purchase inspection, or we can drop it off for you.

I don't see the car I want.+

Text us the make, model, budget and must-haves. We buy at auction every week and can usually find it within 2–3 weeks.
