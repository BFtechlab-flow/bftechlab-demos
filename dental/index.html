FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

Do you take my insurance?+

We're in network with most PPO dental plans. Add your plan to your appointment request and we'll confirm your coverage before you come in.

How soon can I be seen?+

Routine visits are usually within one to two weeks. For pain or a broken tooth, call us in the morning and we'll try to see you the same day.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?+

Absolutely. Tell us in your request and we'll book extra time, explain each step and agree on a signal for when you need a break.

Where do I park?+

There's free parking right behind the building, and the entrance is on the ground floor with no steps.
