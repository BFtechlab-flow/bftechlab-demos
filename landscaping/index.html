    Do I have to sign a contract?+

    No. Weekly plans are month to month. Pause for vacation or cancel any time with a week's notice.

    Do I need to be home?+

    No. Just leave gates unlocked and dogs inside. We text you a photo when we're done.

    Are your products safe for pets and kids?+

    We offer organic and pet-safe options for every treatment, and we always flag the yard after spraying.

    What areas do you serve?+

    Brookside and towns within 15 miles, including Oak Ridge, Riverton, Fairview and Hillcrest.
