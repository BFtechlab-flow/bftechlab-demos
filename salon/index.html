FAQ

Questions we hear every day

Which service should I book?+

If you're unsure, book a free consultation. Your stylist will recommend the service and give you a price.

What's your cancellation policy?+

Please give us 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations and no-shows are charged 50% of the service.

Do you do patch tests for color?+

Yes. New color clients are asked to come in for a quick patch test at least 48 hours before.

Can I buy the products you use?+

Yes. Your stylist will recommend what you need, and nothing you don't.

Do you sell gift cards?+

Yes, in any amount, at the front desk or by phone.
