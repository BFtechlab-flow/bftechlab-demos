FAQ

Questions we hear every day

Is the consultation really free?+

Yes. It's a 20-minute appointment with a provider, with no charge and no obligation to book a treatment.

Does it hurt?+

Most treatments are very comfortable. We use numbing cream and ice for injectables and tell you what to expect at every step.

How much downtime should I expect?+

Facials and wrinkle relaxers have none. Fillers and peels may cause mild redness or swelling for a day or two.

Who performs the treatments?+

Licensed nurses, nurse practitioners and estheticians, under the supervision of our medical director.

Do you offer payment plans?+

Yes, we offer monthly payment options for larger treatment plans, subject to approval.
