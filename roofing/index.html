FAQ

Questions we hear every day

Will my insurance pay for a new roof?+

If the damage comes from a covered event like hail or wind, often yes. We document everything and meet your adjuster so nothing is missed.

How long does a replacement take?+

Most homes are done in one day, two for larger or more complex roofs. We tarp and clean up every evening.

Do you install over the old shingles?+

No. We always tear off down to the deck so we can replace any rotten wood and give you a full warranty.

Do you offer financing?+

Yes, from 0% APR for 12 months or low monthly payments up to 15 years, subject to approval.

What does the warranty cover?+

Our workmanship for 25 years, plus the manufacturer's material warranty. Both transfer if you sell the house.
