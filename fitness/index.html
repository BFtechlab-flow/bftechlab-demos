FAQ

Questions we hear every day

I'm a complete beginner. Is that okay?+

Absolutely. Every new member gets a free intro session, and every class has easier options. Half our members started from zero.

Is there a contract?+

No. Plans are month to month. Annual plans are optional and save you two months.

Can I freeze my membership?+

Yes, up to three months a year for travel, injury or anything else, at no charge.

How much is personal training?+

Packs start at $65 per session, or choose the Coached plan for four sessions a month plus unlimited classes.

Do you have childcare?+

Yes, weekday mornings from 8 to 11 am, for kids 6 months to 10 years. $5 per visit for members.
