FAQ

Questions we hear every day

How much does a lawyer cost?+

Nothing upfront. We work on a contingency fee: we're paid a percentage of what we recover, and only if we win.

Should I talk to the other driver's insurance company?+

It's best to talk to a lawyer first. Adjusters are trained to minimize your claim, and what you say can be used against you.

How long will my case take?+

Many cases settle in 6 to 18 months. Cases that go to trial can take longer. We'll give you an honest timeline.

What is my case worth?+

It depends on your injuries, medical bills, lost wages and how the accident happened. A free review gives you a realistic range.

Is there a deadline to file?+

Yes. Most states give you about two years from the date of the injury, sometimes less. Don't wait to get advice.
