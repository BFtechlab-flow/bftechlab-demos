FAQ

Questions we hear every day

Do you charge a trip fee?+

There's a $49 diagnostic fee, which is waived if you go ahead with the repair. Home Club members never pay it.

Are your plumbers licensed?+

Yes. Every team is led by a licensed master plumber, and the company is fully insured.

Do you charge more on weekends?+

Saturdays are the same price as weekdays. Nights, Sundays and holidays have a flat $95 emergency fee.

Can I pay over time?+

Yes. Water heaters and bigger jobs can be financed over 12 to 60 months, subject to approval.

What does the warranty cover?+

Labor on every repair for one year. New water heaters also carry the manufacturer's 6 to 12-year tank warranty.
